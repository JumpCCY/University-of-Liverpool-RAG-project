Located on Brownlow Hill in the University’s city centre campus, Crown Place provides self-catered ensuite accommodation to 1,254 students.‎

Please note that we have received an extremely high number of applications for Crown Place already for 2026 entry - so we have removed it as a preference on the accommodation portal to manage expectations as it is effectively sold out.

Crown Place offers a choice of either single rooms, 3/4 deluxe rooms, deluxe studios and large studio apartments.

There are also accessible rooms available for students with mobility issues, as well as facilities for students requiring live-in carer support.

In shared areas you can enjoy a complimentary cleaning service, helping to keep your kitchen and lounge area clean and tidy for you to enjoy. Shared kitchens offer all the self-catering facilities you need.

Residential advisers

Student welfare within our halls of residence are the responsibility of hall wardens, assisted by senior residential advisers and the wider residential adviser team.

Wardens are senior members of staff who live at the accommodation, ensuring students get the support and assistance they need throughout their time with us. At Crown Place, there is a warden who oversees all aspects of pastoral care.

The team are there to help you with any personal problems and aim to assist students by being pastoral leaders. You will usually meet your warden welcome events during the first few days of term: it is important that you attend these, as there will be information specific to your halls.

Our residential adviser team provides an on-call emergency service Monday to Friday, 8pm to 7.30am and Saturday to Sunday, 6pm to 9am.
