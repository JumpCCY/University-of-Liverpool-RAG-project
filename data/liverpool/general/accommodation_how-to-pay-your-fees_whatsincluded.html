What is included in the cost of your accommodation?

The cost of your accommodation covers lots of useful things.

The cost includes:

  • All fuel bills (such as gas, water and electricity)
  • Basic contents insurance provided by Howden's - it is important to register your Student Cover here
  • High-speed internet facility which connects to the University file stores and your online teaching modules
  • WiFi is included all around each hall - IT Services can be accessed here
  • Exemption from council tax - Council Tax student certificates can be requested from Student Administration here
  • Membership of the Halls Student Committee, which gives you a voice in how your halls are monitored and the events you take part in
  • Halls Life events such as up to 75% off organised trips through the Guild's "give it a go" programme
  • Personal support from our in-house Residential Advisor team and pastoral support from our senior academic wardens
  • Onsite laundry is paid for separately outside of your rent and is operated by Circuit Laundry. The cost for 2026 entry is £2.50 for a wash and £1.50 for a dry
