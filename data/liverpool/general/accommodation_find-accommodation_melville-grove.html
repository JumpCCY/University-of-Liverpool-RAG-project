Melville Grove is a popular gated residence on the University's city centre campus, providing accommodation for undergraduate first year students.

All rooms are fully furnished and high speed broadband-connected, with shared kitchens and washing facilities.

In shared areas, you can enjoy a complimentary cleaning service to help keep your kitchen, lounge and bathroom clean and tidy.

City centre campus facilities, including the sports centre, libraries and Guild of Students, are close by. There are also plenty of local bars, cafés and shops in the area for you to enjoy.

Residential advisers

Student welfare within our accommodation is the responsibility of hall wardens, assisted by senior residential advisers and the wider residential adviser team.

Wardens are all senior members of staff who live in the halls of residence, ensuring students get the support and assistance they need throughout their time in University accommodation. At Melville Grove, we have a warden who oversees all aspects of pastoral care.

The team are there to help you with any problems and assist students by being pastoral leaders. You will usually meet your warden at welcome events during the first few days of term: it is important that you attend these events as there will be information specific to your hall of residence.

Our residential adviser team provides an on-call emergency service Monday to Friday, 8pm to 7.30am and Saturday to Sunday, 6pm to 9am.
