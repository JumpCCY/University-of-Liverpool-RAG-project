Study Abroad students / Postgraduate Students

Tudor Close is a small accommodation site on the University's city centre campus, home to approximately 100 students taking part in our Study Abroad programme.

Most of the accommodation available at Tudor Close are single study bedrooms, organised into small groups around shared kitchens and washing facilities.

In shared areas, you can enjoy a complimentary cleaning service to keep your kitchen and lounge area clean and tidy for you to enjoy.

The residence is gated and there are common spaces where you can socialise. Campus facilities, including the sports centre, Guild of Students and libraries, are all close by.

Residential advisers

Student welfare within our accommodation is the responsibility of hall wardens, assisted by senior residential advisers and the wider residential adviser team.

Wardens are all senior members of staff who live in the halls of residence, ensuring students get the support and assistance they need throughout their time in University accommodation. At Tudor Close, we have a warden who oversees all aspects of pastoral care.

The team are there to help you with any problems and assist students by being pastoral leaders. You will usually meet your warden at welcome events during the first few days of term: it is important that you attend these events as there will be information specific to your hall of residence.

Our residential adviser team provides an on-call emergency service Monday to Friday, 8pm to 7.30am and Saturday to Sunday, 6pm to 9am.
