Living in Liverpool

Famed for its revolutionary music, sport and culture, Liverpool is a city with heart, passion and a desire to be at the core of development and success. And with over 50,000 students in the city, it’s the perfect place to come and study in a friendly, globally-relevant city. Become part of a safe and diverse community and discover a city renowned for its warm embrace and forward-thinking attitude.

Student life in Liverpool

When you choose to come and study in Liverpool, you get access to more than just a great opportunity to build your future potential. You get to live in a city that marches to the beat of its own drum and carves its own path. Explore the endless supply of things to see and do in Liverpool that make it the perfect place to live as a student, from live music to nightlife and everything between.

Art, museums and festivals

Discover a range of museums, galleries and music festivals. From The Beatles Story to Liverpool Sound City, the city is the perfect place for creatives to indulge themselves.

Nightlife and live music

Enjoy nights out at over 250 city centre bars, pubs and clubs. You’re sure to find a spot to suit your taste. Find the ideal place for a night on the dance floor or live much with new friends in Liverpool.

Theatre and performance

Discover large performance venues and intimate theatres, or cinemas screening independent and mainstream films. If you’re a fan of theatre and performance, Liverpool has something for you.

Relaxing and eating out

Find cafés, bistros and restaurants to suit all tastes and budgets. Liverpool is also home to a wealth of beautiful green spaces where you can unwind with friends or for quiet reflection.

Shopping

Liverpool is a shopping destination to rival any city. You can find everything from large shopping centres and big brands to independent boutiques and vintage places with unique pieces.

Sport

Liverpool is well known for its passion for sport. The city is home to two of the Premier League’s biggest football teams, Liverpool Football Club and Everton Football Club as well as other successful sports stars.

Hidden gems

Find something a little different in Liverpool. Whether it’s the perfect place for a night out, to grab a bite to eat, or to enjoy views of the city, there are many hidden gems to uncover.

Getting to Liverpool

Explore your travel options for getting here. Liverpool is an accessible city with extensive travel options including motorway links and rail networks.

Getting around the city

Find more about travel around Liverpool, a walkable city with lots of travel options to help you get around. Discover extensive rail networks, motorway links, bus routes and cycle lanes, and more.
