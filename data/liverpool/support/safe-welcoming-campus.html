Safe and Welcoming Campus

At the University of Liverpool, we’re committed to building a campus environment where every student feels safe, supported, and respected.

If you’ve experienced or witnessed harassment, sexual misconduct, bullying, hate crime, assault, discrimination, or any behaviour that makes you feel unsafe, uncomfortable, or targeted, we want to know about it.

Together, we can create a respectful and inclusive community for everyone.
