Introducing disability support

Accessing support or adjustments at university can be a little different to school or college. The language and terminology is different, but support is still available for you.

If you had been identified as having 'Special Educational Needs (SEN)' or 'Additional Needs' - or, if you need support for a disability, learning difference, long-term medical condition or mental health condition - the following pages will help you to access the support and adjustments you need.
