Wellbeing Advice and Guidance

Wellbeing practitioners are here to support you with wellbeing advice and guidance.

The Wellbeing Team is staffed with wellbeing practitioners who are here to help you look after your wellbeing and make the most of your time at University. They provide information, advice and guidance to students on a range of personal and social issues, with a focus on improving the overall mental wellbeing of students at The University of Liverpool.

Our Student Wellbeing Practitioners provide confidential, non-judgemental support on campus, and remotely. You can complete our online triage form to access further support. Once completed, we will contact you directly. We offer several forms of support such as Mental Health Clinics, wellbeing activities and workshops and tailored support.

You can feel confident to get in touch with the Wellbeing Team for advice on anything that is worrying you at any time during your studies.
