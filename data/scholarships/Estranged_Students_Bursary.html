Estranged Students Bursary

The University of Liverpool offers a bursary of £1,000 per year to students who are identified as estranged by Student Finance England (Wales, Northern Ireland or the NHS).

You do not need to make a separate application for this bursary but you will need to contact Money Advice and Guidance, who will establish your eligibility with your funding body.

Students who later become estranged whilst studying at the University, can apply to receive the award.

This bursary is for UK Undergraduate students.

Please contact the Wellbeing Team on advice@liverpool.ac.uk.
