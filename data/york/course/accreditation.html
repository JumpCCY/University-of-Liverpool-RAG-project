Our broad and principled approach to computer science will give you a thorough grounding in theory as well as practical skills, helping you to solve real-world problems.

Discover how computer science is shaping the technologies that power our world: Our Computer Science degree combines strong theoretical foundations with practical problem-solving. You'll explore everything from digital hardware and operating systems to programming languages, data and artificial intelligence, developing the knowledge to tackle real-world challenges.

Develop the technical and analytical skills that employers value: You'll learn to design user-centred systems, understand computing across multiple levels of abstraction, harness the power of data, and build intelligent systems that learn from it. Our curriculum is shaped by industry input, ensuring you'll graduate with the knowledge and adaptability to succeed in a rapidly evolving sector.

Study in modern facilities at the heart of our computing community: You'll learn in the purpose-built Ian Wand Building, with access to software and hardware laboratories, collaborative learning spaces and specialist research facilities that support both teaching and world-leading research.

Graduate with industry experience: Add a Placement Year to gain valuable workplace experience, strengthen your CV and put your knowledge into practice before starting your career. This course is also available as a four-year integrated masters (MEng).

  • Computer Science (with a year in industry) (BSc)
  • Computer Science (with a year in industry) (MEng)

  • BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT on behalf of the Engineering Council for the purposes of partially meeting the academic requirement for a Chartered Engineer.
