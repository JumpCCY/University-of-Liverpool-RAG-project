Applying for accommodation

The application portal for entry 2026/27 is now open - you can apply for accommodation now.

Apply now

Please note, the accommodation guarantee is available to all first year undergraduates who apply before 31st August 2026 for the September 2026 entry. The guarantee includes University and partner providers accommodation options.

Take a look at our accommodation and decide on the best fit for you. If you receive an offer from our preferred provider, your tenancy agreement will be with the provider, not the University of Liverpool.

Step by step instructions How to Apply

When to apply

Our accommodation is very popular. We suggest applying right after you get your offer to study at the University of Liverpool or your invite to interview.

Even if you haven't decided yet, still apply as allocations are made based on date of application. It's first-come, first-served, so applying early gives you a better chance of getting one of your preferences, rather than waiting until after making Liverpool your firm or insurance choice. 

Accommodation offers will be sent out by us once you've met the conditions of your offer. Typically, this will happen in August once you've received your A-Level results.

How to apply

  • Once you have your Student ID/Reference Number you can make an application on My Liverpool.  You don't need to wait for an invite to apply
  • Click on "I don't have an MWS account" click "Forgot PIN" and enter your Student ID and date of birth.
  • An email with PIN setup instructions will be sent to your email within 24 hours (check your junk/spam folders)
  • Once your PIN is set, apply for accommodation by logging into My Liverpool and clicking "continue to your application" under the accommodation section.

Apply as soon as you have your offer and the portal is open, don't wait until you have viewed the accommodation.  You can apply and then change your preferences at a later date, this doesn't affect your application date.  By waiting, you may miss out on more popular accommodations.

Room preferences

As part of the application process, you'll be able to select three preferences for your choice of room and halls. Please be aware that preferences are not guaranteed and are offered based in order of date of application. We try our best to allocate you to your preferences, however it is subject to availability.

We check your first preference, if that is gone, your second and then your third.  If we can't provide one of your preferred choices, we'll do our best to offer the next best available room. Please be aware that the pool of rooms we allocate to include both University owned and private partner provider accommodation. Your offer of accommodation may be for a private hall that we are partnered with.

Please note that we have both self-catered and catered rooms within our provision of rooms and you may be offered either of these room types. If you are a first year undergraduate you may be offered a catered room even if this is not one of your preferences in your application.

Please note that we have received an extremely high number of applications for Crown Place already for 2026 entry - so we have removed it as a preference on the accommodation portal to manage expectations.

Medical requirements and reasonable adjustments

If you require specific adjustments to your accommodation or for your learning experience, it is vital that you provide any medical requirements you may have when submitting your application. Find out more about priority rooms.

You can also contact the Disability Support Team to discuss your options. 

Application deadlines

  • First year undergraduate students are guaranteed accommodation at the University of Liverpool or within University Approved accommodation if you apply before the deadline on 31 August 2026
  • Students who hold an Unconditional Offer will receive their offer of accommodation once they have become an ‘expected entry’
  • If you hold an unconditional offer and apply on or after 1st July 2026, you will only receive your offer following A Level Results Day
  • Insurance and clearing undergraduates need to make their application for accommodation as soon as possible. We will house you either within our accommodation or with our private provider depending on their availability.

Returning undergraduate students

We have a limited number of rooms available for second, third and fourth-year undergraduates. Therefore, you will need to get your application in early, as we allocate on a first come first served basis. You will receive an offer of accommodation shortly following your application - please note returning undergraduates are not guaranteed accommodation.

Wherever possible we will house you with all returning undergraduate students. Due to this being a smaller cohort we are usually able to honour requests to live with friends in shared flats also. We send a survey out in the summer before you move in to ask if there are others you would prefer to live with.

You can apply for accommodation once the accommodation portal opens in January. The sooner you apply the more likely you are to get one of your preferences of hall. Due to you already being an expected entrant at the University we are usually able to send you an accommodation offer within a few weeks of you making an application.

We understand that plans can change, so we thought it was right to be as flexible as possible when it comes to booking your accommodation. Booking with us is straight forward, and you do not need to pay a deposit or find a guarantor.

Postgraduate students

September entry

You can apply for accommodation as soon as you have received your offer to study. Once we have received your application and your status at the University of Liverpool is confirmed as an expected entrant, we will send you an offer for accommodation. We are able to offer accommodation to most postgraduate applicants

International postgraduate students are guaranteed accommodation with us for September entry, so long as you apply no later than 31 July 2026. If you apply after this date, we will do our best to secure you good quality accommodation, either in our halls or with one of our preferred providers.

We understand that now more so than ever plans can change, so we thought it was right to be as flexible as possible when it comes to booking your accommodation. Booking with us is straight forward, and you do not need to pay a deposit or find a guarantor. If your plans change later in the year, you can cancel your accommodation and we will not charge you if you do not arrive.

January entry

For postgraduate courses that begin in January - we do not guarantee accommodation however we are usually able to offer an option of accommodation to students who apply. In order to check availability for January start please email us at accommodation@liverpool.ac.uk with your student ID number in November/December time and we can check availability for you. The accommodation fee will still be discounted as above - and start from when you wish to move in until the end of August of that year. Often we can look to extend students whose course date ends in January so that they have accommodation for their full stay - where possible in the same room.

Study Abroad students

After you formally accept your offer to study with us you will be able to apply for University Accommodation via the Accommodation Portal, accessed through your Liverpool Life account.

You will need your student ID number, this is a 9-digit number starting “20” and may be referred to as a reference.  You can find this in your offer letter.

After you visit Liverpool Life, click “Set pin for the first time” and instructions will be sent to you within 24 hours on how to set up your PIN.  When you have logged in, click “Continue to your application” under the accommodation section place an application under 2025-26 and ensure you select the correct application route;

  • Semester 1 only – this is for students who require accommodation in Liverpool for the first semester (September – January). If you are staying for Semester 1 only, you can apply for one of the rooms available in the Study Abroad Villages only (Tudor Close & Melville Grove). Accommodation isn’t guaranteed for Semester 1 students and is limited. You should apply as early as you are able.  Accommodation will be allocated randomly to make the process as fair as possible and offers will be sent out in mid-July once your place is confirmed. We also allocate some rooms outside of the Study Abroad Village once this is filled.
  • Semester 2 only – this is for students who require accommodation from the second semester (January – June). If you are staying for Semester 2 only, you are guaranteed a place in University of Liverpool Accommodation, or with our University Approved Providers. There is no specific deadline to apply for accommodation, however, it is best to apply early to receive one of your preferred halls/rooms.  Where possible you will be placed in the Study Abroad Village, however, you also have options to stay in our other halls such as Crown Place, Philharmonic Court and Vine Court. If you wish to take up your place, apply via Liverpool Life.
  • Full year – This is students who are studying in the Global Opportunities/Study Abroad programme for the full year.  You will be housed on a 39-week contract with first-year undergraduate students. If you are staying for the full academic year, you are guaranteed accommodation in the University of Liverpool accommodation provided you have applied before 31 July

After receiving an accommodation offer you must accept or decline within the deadline.

If you have made other arrangements for accommodation please decline the accommodation offer and notify the accommodation office.

Friendship groups

You can select up to 2 friends to live with in our accommodation, we will try to facilitate this, but it is not guaranteed. We send a survey closer to move in period around friendship groups. Please be aware that we are unable to change room allocations once you have selected the room. Friendship group options are only available to students who book before 31st July 2026.

Students staying on campus for summer

Students who are currently staying within our halls and want to apply for accommodation in the summer can do so on Liverpool Life. You will need to make a request for summer accommodation under the Requests section of your 2025/26 accommodation application, we will then look to send you an offer for the period you specify. You can make a request now via My Liverpool.

Specific room types cannot always be guaranteed. We will start to send out Summer offers in May 2026.

If you are not currently staying in our halls, please email accommodation@liverpool.ac.uk and include your name, student ID number and the dates you require the accommodation.  We won't be allocating or sending offers out until mid to end of May 2026 so we would recommend that you contact us via email in mid-May.

Please complete the application as soon as possible as rooms will be offered on a first-come first-serve basis. Students currently living in our accommodation will receive priority. Where available, we can also try to house you with your friends, although this is not guaranteed.  

Offers for summer accommodation will be sent out from mid May and you will receive your invoice shortly after you check in. Payment is not required until after you received your invoice and can be done online.  

If you have any further queries, please don't hesitate to contact the Accommodation Office.

Accepting your accommodation offer

Accommodation offers will be sent out to students once they've met the conditions of their offer. Typically, this will happen in August once A-Level results have been published.

All students will receive an email notifying them they have been sent an offer to accept. To accept your offer of accommodation:

  1. Log into Liverpool life
  2. Enter your date of birth
  3. Click ‘accept’.

It is important to make sure you check through the terms and conditions as you are signing up to our Student Accommodation Licence.

Cooling off period

You have a seven-day 'cooling off period' to cancel your Student Accommodation Licence. Cancelling after this period may result in liability for the full dates specified on your Licence, starting from the date you accepted it.

Rejecting your accommodation offer

It's highly advised to accept your offer. If it expires or is rejected, you lose the guarantee, and we might not provide an alternative. Instead, you'll be placed on a waiting list without a guaranteed new room offer. If you are unhappy with the room we have offered, you can place yourself on a room move waiting list. However you will need to accept your current offer to be placed on this list.

Fire Safety Works Statement

We take our residents’ safety very seriously, and you might be aware there have been recent changes to Fire Safety Regulations in high-rise buildings affecting many student accommodation properties in the UK.

In 2026/27 there will be a programme of fire safety improvement works conducted at Greenbank Student Village.  The programme of work will include some maintenance to bedroom doors, communal doors and riser doors throughout all buildings located on Greenbank Student Village.  Additionally, there will be some work carried out to the void spaces above some ceilings and will form part of the overall fire safety programme.  Following completion of the maintenance to all fire doors, this will then be followed up with any painting that’s required to make good from the maintenance of those areas.

It is anticipated that the fire safety maintenance work including any remedial painting will take up to 5 working days per cluster (corridor that accommodates a specific number of bedrooms, communal hall and kitchen).   We expect that there will be some noise levels from the fire safety maintenance work and may include intermittent drilling etc. and we are working closely with the programme leads to minimise disruption and impact on students during this time. If the fire safety works require access to your bedroom, the Greenbank Team will contact you to make arrangements. You will receive a minimum of 7 days' notice before the works are due to take place.

Our buildings and systems already meet all safety requirements, this is just part of our ongoing efforts to keep our accommodation in top condition. The buildings remain suitable for occupation, and appropriate fire safety arrangements will continue to be maintained while these planned improvement works are completed; this is a long-term precaution to ensure the building continues to maintain and improve the building’s fire safety measures and support continued compliance with current standards.
