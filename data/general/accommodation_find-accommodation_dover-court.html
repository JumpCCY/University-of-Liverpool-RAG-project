Dover Court

From: £204.33 per week

Returning undergraduates | Postgraduate students

Dover Court is located just to the north of the University's city centre campus, and offers self-catered, en-suite accommodation.

All rooms are fully furnished, broadband-connected and have en-suite bathrooms, with five to six student rooms clustered around shared social kitchens and living rooms. A complimentary cleaning service is offered in all shared areas.

There is a large, quiet communal study space at Dover Court - ideal if you need to bring your work home with you. Campus facilities are all located close by, and there are plenty of bars, cafés and shops on offer locally.

Returning undergraduate students are able to apply individually or as a friendship group for shared accommodation with up to six flatmates.

We also offer accommodation at Dover Court to postgraduate students on a 51 week contract, with the option of extending and staying in the same room for the duration of your time at Liverpool.

Residential advisers

Student welfare within our accommodation is the responsibility of hall wardens, assisted by senior residential advisers and the wider residential adviser team.

Wardens are all senior members of staff who live in the halls of residence, ensuring students get the support and assistance they need throughout their time in University accommodation. At Dover Court, we have a warden who oversees all aspects of pastoral care.

The team are there to help you with any problems and assist students by being pastoral leaders. You will usually meet your warden at welcome events during the first few days of term: it is important that you attend these events as there will be information specific to your hall of residence.

Our residential adviser team provides an on-call emergency service Monday to Friday, 8pm to 7.30am and Saturday to Sunday, 6pm to 9am.
