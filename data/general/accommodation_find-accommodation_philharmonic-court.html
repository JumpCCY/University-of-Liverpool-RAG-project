Philharmonic Court offers self-catered en-suite rooms for students, all with double beds, arranged round a private central courtyard, with a 24 hour reception.

Philharmonic Court also features its own social spaces, on-site laundry facilities and study room. All rooms are fully furnished, broadband-connected and finished to a high standard.

In all shared areas, you can enjoy a complimentary cleaning service to keep your kitchen and lounge areas clean and tidy for you to enjoy.

City centre campus facilities are close by, including the sports centre, libraries and Guild of Students. There are plenty of local bars, cafés and shops in the area.

Residential advisers

Student welfare within our accommodation is the responsibility of hall wardens, assisted by senior residential advisers and the wider residential adviser team.

Wardens are all senior members of staff who live in the halls of residence, ensuring students get the support and assistance they need throughout their time in University accommodation. At Philharmonic Court, we have a warden who oversees all aspects of pastoral care.

The team are there to help you with any problems and assist students by being pastoral leaders. You will usually meet your warden at welcome events during the first few days of term: it is important that you attend these events as there will be information specific to your hall of residence.

Our residential adviser team provides an on-call emergency service Monday to Friday, 8pm to 7.30am and Saturday to Sunday, 6pm to 9am.
