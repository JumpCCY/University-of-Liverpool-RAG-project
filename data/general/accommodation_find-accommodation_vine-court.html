Located on the University's city centre campus, Vine Court offers self-catered and catered accommodation.

Study bedrooms are all en-suite, with a high speed broadband connection and shared social kitchen/diner and lounge areas. In our shared areas, you can enjoy a complimentary cleaning service to keep your kitchen and lounge area clean for you to enjoy.

Vine Court has an excellent pay as you go on-site restaurant, plus a launderette, large student chill-out mezzanine area and outdoor spaces for socialising.

City centre campus facilities are close by, including the sports centre, libraries and Guild of Students. A variety of local shops are just a few minutes’ walk away.

Vine Court is also has our catered accommodation option offering a flexible service available in Bertie and Bella the onsite restaurant, and also at outlets across campus. You can find out more about how catered rooms work here: Vine Court Catered Plan 26-27

Vine Court is Going Greener 🌍

The University is ambitious. We are aiming for a climate-resilient campus and to achieve net-zero carbon by 2035. This means we aim to have minimal negative and maximum positive environmental impact.

We monitor energy and carbon across the entire University as part of our Climate plan. Tracking our progress supports our Sustainability Strategy and Net-Zero carbon targets.

Here at Vine Court, we are currently upgrading the heating and hot water systems as part of a project to decarbonise your accommodation.

✅ Heating and hot water will soon come from low-carbon air source heat pumps

✅ Helping us cut carbon emissions, saving around 262tCO2/yr, and fight climate change

✅ Supports our path to Net-Zero by 2035
✅ Creating a more sustainable future for your campus

Thank you for your patience while the works take place. Together, we’re making your home more energy-efficient, comfortable, and environmentally friendly.

For further information about our plans, refer: Carbon | About us | University of Liverpool

Residential advisers

Student welfare within our accommodation is the responsibility of hall wardens, assisted by senior residential advisers and the wider residential adviser team.

Wardens are all senior members of staff who live in the halls of residence, ensuring students get the support and assistance they need throughout their time in University accommodation. At Vine Court, we have a warden who oversees all aspects of pastoral care.

The team are there to help you with any problems and assist students by being pastoral leaders. You will usually meet your warden at welcome events during the first few days of term: it is important that you attend these events as there will be information specific to your hall of residence.

Our residential adviser team provides an on-call emergency service Monday to Friday, 8pm to 7.30am and Saturday to Sunday, 6pm to 9am.
