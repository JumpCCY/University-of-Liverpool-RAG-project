Shopping

Liverpool is a shopping destination to rival any city.

 

High street

In the city centre, Liverpool ONE houses 160 shops, restaurants to suit all tastes and a multiplex cinema. It received a ‘Retail Icon Award’ from Liverpool ECHO in 2018.

Upmarket

Nearby, there is high-end and designer fashion at the Met Quarter where you can find brands such as Hugo Boss, Kurt Geiger, and Jo Malone.

Vintage and independent

Liverpool has more than its share of small, eclectic boutiques and quirky shops in Bold Street, which offers a range of styles from urban street wear to vintage.

The Baltic Triangle offers a selection of vintage finds. Head over to the Red Brick Market in Cains Brewery. There you will find unique homeware, retro clothing, and everything in-between!

The University also regularly hosts vintage kilo sales where clothes can be bought for £15 per kilogram.
