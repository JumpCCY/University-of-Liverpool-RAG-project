Facilities and environment

Facilities

You'll be part of the School of Computing, based in the Urban Sciences Building on our Newcastle Helix campus.

This brand new £58m building offers great facilities for our students, including:

  • cyber-physical systems laboratory
  • decision theatre for data visualisation
  • flat floor teaching facilities, designed for teamwork, interactive learning and industry-style collaboration
  • hundreds of high-spec computers, with access to powerful resources and software

The building and the surrounding area is a living laboratory, underpinning research to make urban centres more sustainable for future generations.

Explore the Urban Sciences Building

The Newcastle Helix is an innovation district in the heart of Newcastle-upon-Tyne.

Research across the Newcastle Helix focuses on:

  • cyber-physical systems
  • infrastructure
  • smart grids
  • future of our city
  • big data
