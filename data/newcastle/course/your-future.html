Further study

This degree provides a pathway into Master's and PhD level study in a wide range of fields, depending on your choice of optional modules.

As a Computer Science graduate, you can focus on advanced research in areas such as:

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: Advancing automation, neural networks, and data-driven decision-making.
  • Cyber Security and Cryptography: Protecting digital systems and developing secure communication methods.
  • Data Science and Big Data Analytics: Extracting insights from massive datasets to drive innovation.
  • Human-Computer Interaction (HCI): Designing intuitive and user-friendly digital experiences.
  • Software Engineering: Improving software design, development, and reliability.
  • Robotics and Autonomous Systems: Building intelligent machines for industries like healthcare and manufacturing.
  • Computational Biology and Bioinformatics: Using algorithms to analyse biological data and drive medical advancements.
  • Quantum Computing: Exploring next-generation computing power for complex problem-solving.
  • Internet of Things (IoT): Developing smart, interconnected technologies for real-world applications.
  • Virtual and Augmented Reality: Shaping the future of digital environments and immersive experiences.

Careers support

You’ll graduate with the practical skills, industry experience and confidence to progress straight into the workplace - equipped with the knowledge and attributes employers value most. The School of Computing is supported by the University Careers Service and Learning Partnerships team to ensure you are career-ready. You’ll benefit from:

  • guest lectures from alumni and industry built into modules
  • career-planning sessions and one-to-one support from a dedicated Computing Careers Consultant
  • employer engagement through on-campus events and workshops
  • access to the Computing Employability Hub, featuring opportunities, insights, and vacancies

As well as activities throughout the year, the school hosts a week of career events. In the past, these have included:

  • hackathon workshops
  • placement discussions
  • coding and enterprise challenges
  • employer talks
  • networking events

Our Careers Service is one of the largest and best in the country, and we have strong links with employers. We provide an extensive range of opportunities to all students through our ncl+ initiative.

Preparing you for career success

Computing graduates are in strong demand across almost every sector. As organisations become increasingly digital, the need for skilled software developers, data specialists and technology professionals continues to grow.

With a degree in Computing, you’ll be well prepared for roles in a wide range of technical roles across different industries. Graduates work in specialist tech companies and start-ups, as well as in global organisations across finance, healthcare, manufacturing, retail and the public sector - anywhere technology drives innovation and change.

Employability is embedded throughout your degree, with opportunities to:

  • Connect with alumni and employers each year
  • Gain practical experience through guest lectures, workshops, and industry challenges
  • Develop professional networks and career-focused skills
