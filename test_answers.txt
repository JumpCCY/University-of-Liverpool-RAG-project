Question: is there a machine learning module?
Answer: **Yes** — there is a dedicated machine learning module.

- **COMP219 Advanced Artificial Intelligence** — year 2, optional. This provides an introduction to machine learning, covering traditional algorithms, deep learning, and probabilistic graphical models. It also addresses the safety, security, and trustworthiness of ML in real-world deployment.

Question: what cyber security modules are there?
Answer: **Yes** — there are several modules covering cyber security, ranging from core technical foundations to advanced topics like quantum cryptography.

- **COMP232 Cyber Security** — year 2, optional. Covers cryptographic algorithms, systems vulnerabilities, network and web security, with practical sessions.
- **COMP345 Quantum Computing and Security** — year 3, optional. Focuses on quantum computing threats to classical cryptography and new opportunities in quantum cryptography.
- **COMP211 Computer Networks** — year 2, optional. Includes an identification of critical network security issues and approaches to addressing them.
- **PSYC327 Cyberpsychology-Human Computer Interaction** — year 3, optional. Covers cybercrime topics such as cyberbullying and cyberdating abuse, alongside digital identity and deception.

Security runs through the degree via these optional choices in years 2 and 3, allowing students to specialise further if they choose the **Cyber Security pathway**.

Question: what's COMP305 about?
Answer: **COMP305 Biocomputation** — year 3, optional, 15 credits. It covers biology-inspired adaptive algorithms, specifically **Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs)** and **Genetic Algorithms (GAs)**. Students learn how these are used in pattern recognition, speech recognition, financial modelling, and optimisation problems across science and engineering. The module also places the field in historical and scientific context to help students keep up-to-date with developments.
