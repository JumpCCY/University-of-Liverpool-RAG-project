Question: what cyber security modules are there?

Question: do you teach game development?
Answer: **Yes** — game development is a core part of the optional modules available in years 2 and 3.

- **COMP222 Principles of Computer Games Design and Implementation** — year 2, optional. Covers software architecture, AI techniques, and 3D content creation. Students build a simple 3D video game using an engine.
- **COMP342 Advanced Topics in Computer Game Development** — year 3, optional. Focuses on advanced concepts like game AI, content generation, graphics, physics, and sound.

Game design is introduced as a key area of engagement in year 2, allowing students to deepen their skills with advanced development topics in year 3.

Question: any modules on databases?
Answer: **Yes** — there is a dedicated database module, plus several others that cover data handling and analysis.

- **COMP207 Database Development** — year 2, compulsory. Covers concurrency, security, heterogeneous data sources, semi-structured data, non-relational databases, and large-scale data analysis.
- **COMP108 Data Structures and Algorithms** — year 1, compulsory. Fundamental concepts of algorithm design and implementation using appropriate data structures.
- **COMP337 Data Mining and Visualisation** — year 3, optional. In-depth look at current research issues in data mining, including industrial applications like Google search and IBM Watson.
- **COMP324 Network Mining and Analysis** — year 3, optional. Uses combinatorial and algorithmic techniques to extract information from complex network structures.
- **COMP304 Knowledge Representation and Reasoning** — year 3, optional. Formal ways to reason about knowledge and uncertain or partial information.
- **COMP318 Ontologies and Semantic Web** — year 3, optional. Principles of representing and retrieving knowledge on the Web, including semantic annotation and querying.

Database study starts with core data structures in year 1, moves to dedicated database development in year 2, and expands into advanced data mining and analysis options in year 3.

Question: what's COMP305 about?
Answer: **COMP305 Biocomputation** — year 3, optional, 15 credits. It covers biology-inspired adaptive algorithms, specifically **Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs)** and **Genetic Algorithms (GAs)**. The module also places the field in historical and scientific context to help students keep up-to-date with developments.
